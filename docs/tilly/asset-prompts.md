# Image generation prompts

This describes the Tilly example. Read it only when retaining or changing this
feature; it does not define requirements for a different app.

The sample assets were made with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool.
The first three are independent generations. The art variants use the generated cat
as its edit reference, not a personal photo. No third-party reference image was
submitted. Keep these prompts with the example assets when adapting the template.

## Neighbourhood (`street.png`)

Use case: illustration-story. Create a finished wide landscape illustration for Tilly, a playful iPhone social app about meeting neighbourhood cats on walks. A charming quiet residential street with garden walls, a small park path and three cats hiding in plain sight: one black-and-white cat on a low wall, one ginger cat beside a gate, one cream cat in the grass. Tactile cut-paper and lightly painted editorial illustration, sophisticated simple silhouettes, subtle grain, generous composition, natural anatomy, warm character. Muted sage and deep forest green, sky blue, pale pink houses, small golden accents. Clearly illustrated, never photographic. Wide composition around 3:2, full bleed, all important subjects comfortably inside the frame. No people, no lettering, no app screens, no logos, no map labels, no gradients, no drop shadows. Crisp professional consumer-brand art with specific little neighbourhood details rather than a generic stock illustration.

## Fictional cat (`cat.png`)

Use case: illustration-story. Create a polished portrait illustration of a fictional friendly black-and-white tuxedo cat with green eyes and a pink nose, sitting on a sage green garden wall with a simple pale blue sky and a small leafy branch. This is the sample cat for Tilly, an iPhone cat-sharing social app. Sophisticated tactile cut-paper and gouache editorial style, matte pigment, soft paper grain, simple clear shapes and expressive but anatomically plausible face. Cat fills most of the portrait, entire ears visible, chest and front paws visible, lovely clear silhouette. Palette: black, soft white, forest green and muted sky blue. Portrait 3:4 composition. Clearly a generated illustration, absolutely no photography, no text, no letters, no logos, no user interface, no border, no rounded frame, no gradients, no drop shadows.

## Garden collection (`friends.png`)

Use case: illustration-story. A finished landscape editorial illustration for Tilly, an iPhone social app for meeting neighbourhood cats. Three different fictional cats resting together in a little garden: a long ginger cat stretched across the foreground, a round grey tabby sitting behind, a small cream cat looking around a plant pot. Gentle humorous personalities, cats with realistic proportions interpreted through sophisticated cut-paper and gouache shapes, matte paper grain. Dense, inviting composition with leaves, a soft pink garden wall and deep green plants, no humans. Palette of sage, deep green, muted sky blue, soft pink and warm gold. Wide 3:2 composition, full bleed, heads and ears stay comfortably inside frame. Clearly illustrated, not a photo. No lettering, no labels, no logos, no UI, no decorative starbursts, no gradients, no shadows around image.

## Cubist-art edit (`cubist-cat.png`)

Reference: the generated `cat.png` above. Generated with the built-in image tool;
used on the paywall and marketing page.

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: the art half of a before/after pair for
Tilly's native paywall and marketing page. Reimagine the supplied fictional
black-and-white tuxedo cat as a bold Picasso-inspired cubist painting. Preserve
the same recognizable cat, seated pose, green eyes, pink nose, white chest and
front paws, garden wall, upper-left leafy branch, full ears, portrait crop and
overall subject placement so a crossfade aligns. Make the new image unmistakably
different: fractured angular planes, a dramatically asymmetric cubist face with
simultaneous viewpoints, simplified expressive contours, flattened interlocking
shapes, visible painted canvas texture, saturated cobalt, ochre, coral and
turquoise accents. It must look like a finished original cubist artwork, not a
subtle filter, pixel art or photograph. Keep one cat and the same setting; no
extra subjects. Portrait 3:4, full bleed. No text, signatures, logos, borders or
user interface. Do not reproduce any existing painting.
